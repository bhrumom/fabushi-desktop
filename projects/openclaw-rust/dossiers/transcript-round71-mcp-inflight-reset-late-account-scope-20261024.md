## R71-MCP-OLD-FETCH-MIGRATION-43 — disposed manager's outstanding account display can still scope/persist

2. Thus **after** root clear, the old manager may reintroduce A-scoped settings under the stated in-flight/precondition ordering. This is distinct from Round70's already-identified ordinary list-time migration: here the missing guard is `dispose` versus a pending account-display continuation.
