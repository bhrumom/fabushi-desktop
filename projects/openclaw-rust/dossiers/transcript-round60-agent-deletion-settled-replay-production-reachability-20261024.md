**Conclusion:** At source level, after successful deletion of Agent A and before a Host controller is destroyed or the bounded cache evicts R, replay of an already settled R can produce a success-shaped acknowledgement with a wrong Agent/entry/valid resolution. A still-live pending R may be expired by forget_agent and enter that same cache. This is a *false-positive idempotent acknowledgement*, NOT proof of unauthorized tool execution, replay-caused Settings mutation, authenticated cross-account access or a real-world incident. A deletion flow that drains its pending asks earlier must be separately checked.
