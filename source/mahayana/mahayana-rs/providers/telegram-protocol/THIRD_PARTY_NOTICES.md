### MTProto handshake and cryptography

`src/handshake.rs` uses handshake constructor identifiers from the exact
`mtproto_api.tl` blob. Its pinned safe-prime constant is mapped to
`td/telegram/DhCache.cpp@eb9a4f3ea83fe5223c588f9d304b115a9bcefcf3`.
Its main/test RSA public keys are mapped to the PKCS#1 PEM values in
`td/telegram/net/PublicRsaKeySharedMain.cpp@abdbff5970e2692f19b27506ebcd358401b29d56`;
the Rust representation normalizes those keys to modulus plus exponent.
Handshake behavior was independently implemented in Rust with
`td/mtproto/Handshake.cpp@35c483f6d3ee17c1b56c26bafb6e8fe2c961e55d`
and
`td/mtproto/DhHandshake.cpp@ca410d395f61afd831200ce911a635b15b89ec2b`
as source references.

`src/crypto.rs` is a source-informed independent Rust MTProto 2.0
implementation. Its KDF/key-IV mapping is anchored to
`td/mtproto/KDF.cpp@36daac0b1538cea89b897cc81ebb889182781eb4`
and its message-key/envelope behavior to
`td/mtproto/Transport.cpp@d23173e15db3abdfff6310e8daced15ab42a9761`.
`src/session.rs` maps the accepted server message-id time windows to
`td/mtproto/AuthData.cpp@56c59ae337fbf28a3ec6af2c59eb5c46ca1d4566`.
`src/transport.rs` maps intermediate/padded-intermediate framing and
quick-ack behavior to
`td/mtproto/TcpTransport.cpp@55937def171c8c6aa5d63055beca7403e9507edf`.
No byte-copy claim is made for these Rust implementations.
