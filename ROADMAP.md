## Roadmap policy

For each roadmap item:

1. establish or update a durable Spec;
2. identify architecture/ADR/migration impact;
3. define objective acceptance evidence;
4. implement and verify on an exact source revision;
5. merge only after required gates;
6. release/post-release verify when release is in scope;
7. move completed work out of active status only when its Definition of Done is actually satisfied.

Do not place unverified completion claims or speculative delivery dates here.
